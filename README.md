## Favorite Movie: She's Dating the Gangster

*She's Dating the Gangster* (2014) is a Filipino romantic drama film directed by Cathy Garcia-Molina and adapted from the Wattpad novel by Bianca Bernardino.

### Why It's My Favorite
- Iconic on-screen chemistry between Kathryn Bernardo (Athena) and Daniel Padilla (Kenji)
- A nostalgic 90s setting paired with a modern-day storyline
- A heartwarming and emotional plot twist about true love and sacrifice
